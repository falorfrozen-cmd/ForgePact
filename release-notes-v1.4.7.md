# ForgePact 1.4.7

Bosses can drop their Prime Evil parts more often, the pieces for the Key of
Terror.

## New

- **Prime Evil Parts (Key of Terror).** A new Drop Rates slider on the Loot tab
  makes bosses drop their Prime Evil part more often: Gurag's Soul, Death's
  Sigil, Damien's Eye, Anubis' Ankh, Karp King's Bellybutton and Satan's Horn.
  It uses the game's own roll, on bosses only, and never touches Relics.
  Checked in play: Karp King dropped about 0.7 bellybuttons per kill at x1, 3 at
  x5 and 9 at x35. Above x35 nothing more changes. The infernal parts are
  covered too, where bosses roll them the same way; uber bosses were not tested.
  Off (x1) by default.

## How to update

Download and extract the complete release, then reopen ForgePact. Your existing
settings are retained. Source users can run `Prepare-Plugin.bat` if plugin files
are missing before using **Install Mod Plugin**. The slider is in the panel, but
press **Install Mod Plugin** too: the updated plugin also counts Satan's Infernal
Horn.

Use ForgePact only with an offline / EAC-disabled copy of Hero Siege.
