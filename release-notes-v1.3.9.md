# ForgePact 1.3.9

Works together with Hero Siege Item Editor 2.15.0.

## What's new

- **Custom items can have their own name, a special golden line and a description.**
  You write them in the Item Editor; ForgePact shows them in the game's item tooltip,
  just like on a real unique item.
- **Two new item powers** you can forge in the Item Editor:
  - **Tyrant's Crown** (a helmet). Monsters around you turn rare more often, rares get
    one extra affix, and every rare hunts you down from across the zone. Turn it on with
    the *Tyrant's Crown* switch in the World tab.
  - **Headhunter** (a belt). Kill a rare monster and its affixes become your buffs for
    20 seconds. The stolen affixes float above your character's head with a countdown
    and disappear when you die. Turn it on with the *Headhunter* switch in the World tab.
- **New World setting: Enemy Movement Speed.** Monsters run at you faster (up to +300 %),
  which makes Chaos Tower waves end sooner. By default it works only inside Chaos Tower;
  switch that off to use it everywhere.
- **The Item Editor can now see an item's real stats.** When an item is loaded in the
  game, ForgePact notes its stats so the editor can show and change them.
- **Fix:** when you replace an item's original stats in the editor, old name parts such
  as "Armorer's" no longer stay on the item.
- Nothing changed in monster density, drop rates or special content.

## How to update

Download the zip, unzip it, run ForgePact and press **Install** once. That is all.

Use ForgePact only with an offline / EAC-disabled copy of Hero Siege.
