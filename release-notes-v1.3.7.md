# ForgePact v1.3.7

- **Chaos Tower is back.** It was hidden in v1.3.5 while its Season 10 behaviour
  was verified. The slider works again and towers appear in the world as you explore.
- **Shadow Realm joins Special Content.** Turn the slider up and Shadow Realm
  portals start showing up in newly entered zones, just like Rifts and Battlefields.
- Both use the game's own spawn mechanic. Hero Siege normally rolls for them once
  and then closes the door for the rest of the run; ForgePact reopens that door for
  each new zone. Nothing is hand-placed or faked.
- The installer includes the updated ForgePact plugin.

Set the sliders under **Special Content**, then walk into a zone you have not
visited yet. Zones you already cleared keep whatever they generated at the time.

Use ForgePact only with an offline / EAC-disabled copy of Hero Siege.
