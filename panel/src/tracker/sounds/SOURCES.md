# Bundled alert sounds

The Tracker tab's six bundled alert sounds (#185) are HS Offline Tracker's,
copied byte for byte from `HS-Offline-Tracker/src/assets/sounds/` (MIT, under
the notice in ForgePact's `CREDITS.md`). The Tracker's online-only mail alert
is not carried over. Below is the Tracker's own account of where each one
comes from; the raw sources and the script that processed them stay in the
HS Offline Tracker repository (`scripts/sound-sources/`,
`scripts/generate-alerts.mjs`).

## Project-generated rarity alerts

The five rarity alerts were generated by the project owner with ElevenLabs
Sound Effects on 31 August 2026 and supplied as 48 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV
files. They are not Creative Commons Zero recordings. Their use and
distribution remain subject to the ElevenLabs terms and to the account plan
under which they were generated; the project owner should retain the relevant
subscription and generation records before publishing a release. The owner
cleared them to ship in ForgePact on 2026-10-13 (#185, Q7(c)).

The untouched generated files are retained in HS Offline Tracker under
`scripts/sound-sources/elevenlabs/`. Its `scripts/generate-alerts.mjs`

## Processing

HS Offline Tracker's `npm run generate:sounds` removes confirmed silent tails
and generation-end artifacts, adds click-free fades, loudness-matches the five
rarity alerts to approximately -16 LUFS with -1.5 dBTP headroom, and writes
48 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV files. The rarity alerts remain stereo; the CC0 zone
alert remains mono. No Hero Siege or HS Tracker audio is included in these
files.
